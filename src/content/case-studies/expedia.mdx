<TextBlock>
  I loved this project. It was very much pitch to the client over a few meetings and they left us to get on with it. A fully Android and iOS experience designed and delivered in two months.

  I worked with Expedia to understand what customers wanted and tie it back to the campaign promoting European city destinations. Through research with frequent travellers (usually post uni, not settled down yet, going all over Europe), I found they get fatigued looking up places, checking who flies when. It's a chore.

  The concept: give them the destinations, the dates, rated accommodation, sorted. A win for the company selling more destinations, and a win for the customer who can quickly spot a city break they want to take.
</TextBlock>
