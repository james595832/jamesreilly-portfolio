<TextBlock title="How we worked">
  It would go like this: attend a meeting with leadership, leadership list problems, I step forward with an idea to fix it, go back to the team to scope it, research it and prototype it, then back to the board with the idea, then get engineering to build it.

  I guess we got so much done because we worked in small teams and I had direct access to decision makers. It was easy for me to go back: "Me and this decision will reduce customer calls by 60 percent." Always give detailed numbers to leadership. No one cares about the craft at that level. Harsh but true.

  The work you see here is my direction but carried out by some incredibly talented designers. That was the reason I left. I was not on the tools as much. I left for an individual contributor role rather than a design leadership role.
</TextBlock>
