<TextBlock>
  Virgin Money was moving onto digital, and a lot of business banking was still manual. You visited a branch. Opening a business account took six weeks. Payments were authorised on a physical device. The service barely functioned, and it did not serve customers well.

  I was the lead designer on the business banking journey.
</TextBlock>

<TextBlock title="After login">
  In the first minute, a business owner needs to see the accounts. They also need access for the people who run the company: employees and accountants. And they need payment limits. A payroll officer should be able to pay up to £20,000 without the MD having to authorise it. They need an easier way to see what is coming in and what is going out.
</TextBlock>

<TextBlock title="Adding people">
  Managing users was the tricky problem. The rules decide who can be added, how long they get access, and how you remove them when they leave. They also decide the payment limit, and how you send an account-opening journey to a team member so they can register to use the account.

  I would not drop that. A business cannot run the account if only one person can get in, and it cannot be safe if anyone can pay anything.
</TextBlock>

<TextBlock title="Payments and statements">
  You could not pay properly without the branch, or without that physical device. The app had to change that. Payments online, company cards you can manage, and statements with enough depth to see where the business stands.
</TextBlock>

<TextBlock title="The analytics screen">
  This is the screen I will stand behind. You can see what is coming in and what is going out. There is a VAT and tax estimate, so the business can see that it is earning money and still needs to keep some of it aside.

  It was not straightforward. The tax bands are different across the UK and Ireland, and the estimate still had to be accurate enough to be useful.
</TextBlock>

<TextBlock title="What shipped">
  You could open an account in minutes, with the fraud checks in place. You could pay online, get to banking services quickly if you needed them, manage the company cards, and read a proper statement. The six weeks, the branch, and the lack of staff access were the things the app changed.

  The Capability and Innovation Fund was there to bring money and a lot of business customers into the bank. This app is what made that possible. It was part of a £35 million win.
</TextBlock>
