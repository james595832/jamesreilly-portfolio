<TextBlock>
  I achieved a lot in my time consulting with Virgin, originally Clydesdale Bank. When I joined the bank didn't even have a way to apply online.

  My first project was creating an experience that could be replicated for the suite of products: Current Accounts, Savings, Credit Cards, ISAs. Weeks sitting with architects going back and forth between tech decisions and UX decisions, trying to reach a compromise on what was right for the user but secure for the bank.

  After delivering account opening, I got asked to look at business banking. Virgin Money acquired the bank and everything changed. Business banking was a sleeping giant. Data showed on average it took six weeks plus for a potential customer to go through a business account opening process. Once approved, the app was not mobile optimised. You couldn't make payments without going to a branch. You couldn't add staff to manage day to day finances.

  Me and another designer, Michael Mearns, worked tirelessly with stakeholders to fix these issues. We won a portion of the Capability and Innovation Fund. £35 million.
</TextBlock>
