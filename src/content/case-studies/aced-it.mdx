<TextBlock title="Overview">
  Aced-It is a dedicated interview-preparation platform built exclusively for the design community. In a tech landscape where most interview tools cater heavily to engineering workflows, designers are frequently left behind. Aced-It bridges this gap by offering tailored, interactive preparation that addresses the unique realities of design hiring—from behavioral walk-throughs to the dreaded whiteboard challenge.

<TextBlock title="The Problem & Motivation">
  In the wake of post-pandemic mass layoffs, the design job market has become exceptionally competitive. Job seekers face fragmented application processes, opaque expectations, and little to no tailored practice resources.

  Drawing from years of senior-level hiring and interview experience, the core objective of Aced-It is to give designers a centralized space to gather their materials, sharpen their presentation skills, master interview workflows, and walk into high-stakes interviews fully prepared.
</TextBlock>

<TextBlock title="Key Features & Solution">
  Aced-It is engineered to simulate real-world design evaluation phases:

  **Tailored Practice:** Users can upload their CV and target job descriptions to generate customized interview practice loops.

  **Voice-Powered Simulation:** Candidates practice by actually speaking aloud, receiving automated grading and constructive feedback to rapidly iterate and improve.

  **Whiteboard Challenge Prep:** To conquer the hardest part of the design loop, the platform features 5 distinct whiteboard challenges. Users can ask clarifying questions to set context—and these diagnostic questions are evaluated alongside their final output.
</TextBlock>

<TextBlock title="Validation & Testing">
  The product concept was rigorously vetted and iterated through early-stage concept testing with designer peers, combined with structured user-testing sessions to refine the interactive flows and grading utility.
</TextBlock>

<TextBlock title="Technical Architecture & Stack">

  **Payments & Subscriptions:** Stripe
