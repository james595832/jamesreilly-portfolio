<TextBlock title="The problem">
  Design interviews punish people who can do the work but freeze in the room. Most prep tools are generic question banks or text chatbots. They miss the hard part: speaking clearly under pressure, tying answers to your own experience, and recovering when you drift.
</TextBlock>

<TextBlock title="The product">
  Aced-IT pairs CV context with voice practice. Upload a CV, pick a focus, then rehearse out loud. The system listens, scores the answer against the role and the story on the page, and returns concrete notes — structure, clarity, evidence — so the next take is better.
</TextBlock>

<TextBlock title="Design approach">
  The UI is deliberately quiet: white surfaces, charcoal actions, restrained accent. Interview stress is already high. The product should feel editorial and calm, not like another dashboard screaming for attention.
</TextBlock>
