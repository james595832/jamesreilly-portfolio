<TextBlock>
  One of my more recent freelance projects. A friend who is a leading doctor in Occupational Health needed a revamp of a site that was fine 15 years ago but has not kept up with his offering and brand.
</TextBlock>

<TextBlock title="Beyond a rebrand">
  What started off as a traditional brand project (building a system from a logo up to a full website) became about identifying what his competitors are not offering. Like the ability to book appointments online, send confidential data from companies' HR to OHNI and back securely.

  It was also an opportunity to automate a lot of processes as it's a small operation doing big things.
</TextBlock>
